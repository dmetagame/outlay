# Progress During Hackathon

Outlay grew from a tested smart contract into a live scheduled-payment app with a verified deployment, a real onchain payment, and a narrated demo.

- **21 Sep — Payment contract and accounting foundation.** Built the Solidity payment flow, isolated payment rooms, settlement-caller bounty, and accounting checks. [Contract checkpoint](https://github.com/dmetagame/outlay/commit/2205695).
- **22 Sep — Wallet app and deployment path.** Shipped the React wallet app, payment setup and USDG funding flows, plus a deployment verification kit. The [live app](https://outlay-theta.vercel.app/) is deployed on Robinhood Chain. [App checkpoint](https://github.com/dmetagame/outlay/commit/05b22f2).
- **29 Sep — Deployed payment proven onchain.** Deployed Outlay and completed a real USDG payment: the recipient received 0.10 USDG, the caller received a 0.01 USDG bounty, and the one-time payment closed with no balance remaining. The deployed source has an exact Sourcify match. [Payment receipt and transaction links](../proof/PROOF.md) · [Sourcify match](https://repo.sourcify.dev/4663/0xe1B5d2cF63C43103455ABD802B6B241b959a530c) · [Proof publication checkpoint](https://github.com/dmetagame/outlay/commit/bb4ad4d).
- **29–30 Sep — Settlement desk refined and published.** Reworked the app around payment setup, a settlement queue and clear receipts; checked mobile layouts, wallet states, keyboard access and live contract reads. [Redesign checkpoint](https://github.com/dmetagame/outlay/commit/17280c9) · [wallet and receipt fixes](https://github.com/dmetagame/outlay/commit/c121032).
- **1 Oct — Security and verification strengthened.** Added wallet-runtime and receipt safeguards, expanded audit coverage, and checked the deployed bytecode and payment with read-only verification. The recorded checks include 57 local EVM tests, 10 canonical-USDG fork tests and 9 deployment-verifier tests. [Security checkpoint](https://github.com/dmetagame/outlay/commit/ffd5a9a) · [audit and verification checkpoint](https://github.com/dmetagame/outlay/commit/b1eafd1).
- **1 Oct — Demo completed.** Published a 1080p narrated and captioned walkthrough, with editable project files and a separate transcript. Reconstructed wallet flows are labeled and do not broadcast transactions. [Watch or download the demo](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dmetagame/outlay/main/demo-video/outlay-demo.mp4) · [demo checkpoint](https://github.com/dmetagame/outlay/commit/d9f90c4).
- **2 Oct — Submission assets prepared.** Delivered four 1280×720 project images, including separate views of the real payment evidence; the reconstructed setup image is visibly labeled. [Image checkpoint](https://github.com/dmetagame/outlay/commit/159c8b0).

## Evidence and scope

The live deployment is on Robinhood Chain, chain ID 4663, at `0xe1b5d2cf63c43103455abd802b6b241b959a530c`. The completed settlement was triggered by the sender. Outlay records when a payment is due, but this evidence does not demonstrate an independently operating keeper. The 0.01 USDG bounty demonstrates an onchain payment to the caller; it does not establish profitability after gas. Sourcify reports an exact source match; the Blockscout verified badge remains incomplete.
